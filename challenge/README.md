# 🎮 THE AI COMPANY CHALLENGE

### *"I gave an AI $50. Three days later it had its own company."*
**Hire a team of AI employees and build a real online business in 30 days. No code. No experience.**

> Day 0: no business, no team, no product, no customers.
> Day 30: an AI CEO, a management team, content + sales + product, a live store, and a system you can clone.

This isn't a course. It's a **challenge you play and win** — leveling up from "what's a business?"
to "I run a company staffed entirely by AI." Everything is built tonight using **Cloud AI**
(Claude / ChatGPT) and free no-code tools. If a step needs a coder, it's not in here.

---

## ▶️ Start here

1. Read the 30-second rules below.
2. Open **[Level 0](levels/level-00.md)** and do the build.
3. Each level you **hire a new AI employee** and **unlock** the next.

## The rules (30 seconds)

- **You are the founder. AI is your workforce.** Each level adds an employee to your team.
- **Build, don't study.** Every level ends with a 10–60 min build and a screenshot-able win.
- **No code, ever.** Claude/ChatGPT + Canva, Notion, Gumroad, MailerLite, Make/Zapier.
- **You approve money + publishing.** Largely autonomous, not unattended — that keeps you safe.
- **Done beats perfect.** Post the win. Momentum is the whole game.
