## 1. MARKET RESEARCH

**The wave:** "AI side hustle" is one of the fastest-growing search and content categories. Gen Z
doesn't want a job — they want *leverage*: income that isn't tied to hours. AI just made a one-person
company possible for the first time.

**Who's already selling here, and the gaps:**

| What exists | Why it underdelivers | Our opening |
|---|---|---|
| "Top 10 AI tools" listicles | No path, no outcome | We give a **step-by-step build** |
| $997 guru programs | Hype, gatekept, expensive | We make it **do-it-tonight & affordable** |
| ChatGPT prompt packs | Prompts without a system | We give prompts **inside a company** |
| Generic "learn AI" courses | Feels like school | We make it a **game you win** |

**What makes Gen Z share + buy:**
- **Identity:** "I'm a founder, AI is my team" beats "I'm a student learning a tool."
- **Gamification:** levels, unlocks, and visible progress trigger the same loop as mobile games.
- **Proof you can screenshot:** every level ends with a result you can post.
- **Story hooks:** "I let AI run my business for 7 days" is a video, not a lecture.
- **No code:** the #1 dropout reason is "it got technical." We remove it entirely.

**Conclusion:** the winning product isn't a course — it's a **challenge with a scoreboard**, where
the student builds a real AI-run mini-company and unlocks abilities like leveling up a character.

## 6. LEVEL STRUCTURE (the game frame)

Every level has four game elements, shown as cards:
- **🎯 CHALLENGE** — the question to beat ("Can AI generate a business idea?").
- **🛠️ BUILD** — what you make (an AI employee or asset).
- **🏆 REWARD / UNLOCK** — the new ability you gain.
- **📸 FLEX** — the screenshot-able proof to post.

A persistent **"Company Dashboard"** (a free Notion/Google Sheet template) tracks: employees hired,
assets built, customers, revenue, and current level.

## 7. MISSION STRUCTURE (the 8-beat lesson)

Every level is taught as 8 beats — never explain without showing:

1. **WHY THIS MATTERS** (≤2 min, plain language)
2. **WATCH IT IN ACTION** (a real mini-example with a result)
3. **COPY THIS PROMPT** (ready to paste, self-contained)
4. **PASTE IT INTO CLOUD AI** (exactly where to click / paste)
5. **EXPECTED RESULT** (what you'll see)
6. **BUILD CHALLENGE** (one task, 10–60 min)
7. **RESULT** (what you should now have)
8. **LEVEL COMPLETE** (celebrate + what unlocked)

**Golden rule:** every lesson must contain Example + Prompt + Result. If one is missing, rewrite it.

## 8. AI EMPLOYEE FRAMEWORK (how "hiring" works — no code)

An **AI employee** is just a saved *job description prompt* you can summon anytime. "Hiring" = saving
it. "Onboarding" = pasting your business context once. Three beginner-proof ways to keep your team:

- **Easiest:** a Google Doc / Notion page called **"My AI Company"** with each employee's prompt.
  Copy-paste a prompt into Claude/ChatGPT to "activate" that employee.
- **Better (Claude):** make a **Project** per employee — the role lives in the Project instructions,
  so the employee "remembers" who they are every chat.
- **Better (ChatGPT):** make a **Custom GPT** per employee.

**Every employee prompt uses the same job-card format:**

```
You are my [ROLE] — a senior expert. 
MISSION: [what they own]
CONTEXT ABOUT MY BUSINESS: [paste your 3-line business brief]
HOW YOU WORK: [rules, tone, what to never do]
WHAT I GIVE YOU: [inputs]
WHAT YOU ALWAYS RETURN: [exact output format]
If anything is unclear, ask me one sharp question first.
```

**The org chart you'll build:**

```
                    🧠 AI CEO (sets direction, delegates)
        ┌──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
   📈 Marketing    💰 Sales       📦 Product      🛟 Support
   Director        Director       Director         Lead
   ├ Trend Hunter  ├ Lead Gen     ├ Product        ├ Customer
   ├ Content       ├ Email        │  Creator       │  Support
   │  Creator      │  Agent       └ Analytics      └ Finance
   ├ TikTok Agent  └ ...          
   ├ IG / Shorts   
   └ Growth        
```

You don't build all 16 at once — you **hire one per level**, and by Level 6 you have a management
team that delegates to the specialists.
