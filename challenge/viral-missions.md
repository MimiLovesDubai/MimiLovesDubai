# 🎥 Special Viral Missions

Drop-in side quests designed to be filmed/screenshotted. They're experiments — fun to do, fun to
watch, and they market the Challenge for you. Each is one page: pick one, film it, post it.

> Honest framing: these are *experiments*, not guarantees. You always approve money + publishing.

---

### 1. 🎲 I Let AI Choose My Business
Open Claude/ChatGPT. Prompt: *"Pick a business for me to start this week with €0 and no code — you
decide, I commit."* Whatever it says, you do (Levels 1–3). Film the reveal + the first build.

### 2. 💸 I Let AI Spend My €100 Marketing Budget
Prompt: *"I have €100. Spend it to get my product its first sales — give me an exact plan."* Execute
it. Film the plan, then the results after a week.

### 3. 🤖 I Let AI Run My Company For 7 Days
Run [Level 9](levels/level-09.md). Film a day-by-day vlog of the daily standups and what shipped.
The single most viral format in this whole challenge.

### 4. 🧪 What Would AI Do With €100?
Like #2 but as a pure thought-experiment thread: ask AI for the highest-leverage way to turn €100
into a business, then actually try step 1 on camera.

### 5. 🏁 Can AI Build A Business Faster Than A Human?
Race a friend: you use the AI team, they do it manually. Same 60 minutes. Compare what each of you
ends with. Film the split-screen.

### 6. 🗑️ I Fired My To-Do List And Hired AI Instead
For one week, your only "to-do list" is your AI CEO's daily standup. Document how it feels to be
managed by AI. Surprisingly relatable content.

### 7. 💰 Can AI Make Its First Sale?
Run [Level 7](levels/level-07.md) live. Film from "no store" to the "you made a sale" notification.
The payoff screenshot is the post.

### 8. ⏱️ Can AI Create A Product In One Hour?
Set a 60-minute timer. Run [Level 3](levels/level-03.md). Film the speed-build: idea → finished
product before the timer hits zero.

---

**Posting tip:** start every clip with the result or the question ("I let AI run my business for 7
days…"), not the setup. Hook first, story second. Drop your product/shop link in the bio.
