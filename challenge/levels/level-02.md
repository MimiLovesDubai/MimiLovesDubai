# 🎮 LEVEL 2 — Hire your first digital employee: the Trend Hunter

**🎯 CHALLENGE:** Can AI find hot opportunities *automatically* — what people want right now?
**🏆 REWARD:** Your AI Research Team (Trend Hunter, employee #2).

---

## 1. WHY THIS MATTERS

Selling something nobody wants is the #1 way to waste a month. The Trend Hunter fixes that. It's an
AI employee whose only job is to find what's *trending and selling* in your niche — so you build
things people already want. This is the difference between guessing and *knowing*.

## 2. WATCH IT IN ACTION

You activate the Trend Hunter for "study/productivity digital products." It returns: *aesthetic
Notion dashboards, "that girl" routine planners, exam-revision templates, lock-screen wallpapers
with quotes* — plus the angles blowing up on TikTok this season. Now you're not inventing demand;
you're riding it.

## 3. COPY THIS PROMPT

```
You are my Trend Hunter — a senior trend researcher and social-media analyst on my team.
MISSION: find what's hot, shareable, and selling RIGHT NOW in my niche so I build things 
people already want.
MY BUSINESS: I sell aesthetic study & productivity digital products (Notion templates, 
planners, wallpapers) to Gen Z students. (Replace this line with YOUR business.)

Give me:
1. 7 trending product ideas in my niche, each with a 1-line "why it's hot".
2. 5 content angles/hooks that are going viral around this niche on TikTok/Reels.
3. The 3 ideas with the best money potential for a beginner, and why.
4. One "quick win" product I could create today.
Be specific and current. No fluff. End by asking which idea I want to build first.
```

## 4. PASTE IT INTO CLOUD AI

1. New chat (or your Claude **Project**). Paste the prompt.
2. **Swap the "MY BUSINESS" line** for your own one-liner from Level 1.
3. Send, read the results, reply with the idea you want to build.

> 💡 Make it permanent: in **Claude → Projects → New Project**, name it *"Trend Hunter"* and paste
> the prompt as the project instructions. Now this employee remembers their job every time.

## 5. EXPECTED RESULT

A focused list of trending products + viral hooks + a "build today" quick win. You now have a
shortlist of things people actually want — chosen by data-feel, not vibes.

## 6. BUILD CHALLENGE ⏱️ 20 min

1. Run the Trend Hunter and pick **one quick-win product** to build next level.
2. Save it as **Employee #2** in your MY AI COMPANY doc.
3. Paste your chosen product idea under **"THE BUSINESS → First product"**.

## 7. RESULT

You have an **AI Research Team** and a **validated first product idea** to create in Level 3.
