# 🎮 LEVEL 9 — The AI Company Challenge (7 days of operations)

**🎯 CHALLENGE:** Can your AI workforce run the company for 7 days — with you just approving?
**🏆 REWARD:** AI Business System unlocked.

---

## 1. WHY THIS MATTERS

This is the boss level. Up to now you ran the team. Now the team runs the company, and you become
the *owner* who approves — not the worker who grinds. You'll set up a simple daily loop where your AI
CEO plans, your departments produce, and you spend 15 minutes a day saying "yes" or "tweak this."
This is what "AI runs my business" actually looks like in real life: largely autonomous, with you on
the big decisions.

## 2. WATCH IT IN ACTION

Every morning you open your AI CEO and type *"daily standup."* It gives you: today's 3 tasks, the
content the Content Creator made overnight (drafts ready), the DMs the Sales Agent suggests, and
yesterday's numbers. You approve in 15 minutes. Repeat for 7 days. The business moved every day —
mostly without you.

## 3. COPY THIS PROMPT

```
You are my AI CEO running a 7-DAY AUTONOMOUS OPERATIONS challenge for my company.
MY COMPANY: [paste business + offer + current status]
RULE: you run daily operations; I only approve money decisions and anything published.

Set up the loop:
1. Define a simple DAILY STANDUP format I run with you each morning. It must output:
   - today's top 3 tasks (one per department)
   - content drafts ready to post (from the Content Creator)
   - 3 suggested sales actions (from the Sales Agent)
   - yesterday's numbers to log + one improvement
2. Define an end-of-week REVIEW: what worked, what to drop, next week's focus.
3. Tell me the 2 things I must personally approve before they go live (so I stay safe + legal).
Run DAY 1 now: give me today's standup.
```

## 4. PASTE IT INTO CLOUD AI

1. Use your **AI CEO Project/GPT**. Paste the prompt. Run **Day 1** now.
2. Each morning for 7 days, type **"daily standup"** and work the 15-minute loop.
3. Keep a **7-day log** in MY AI COMPANY (tasks done, posts, sales, lessons).

> ⚠️ **Human-in-the-loop:** you approve anything involving money or publishing. That keeps you safe,
> legal, and in control. "Largely autonomous" — not "unattended."

## 5. EXPECTED RESULT

A repeatable daily standup, a weekly review, and a clear list of what only *you* approve. Run it and
the company operates every day on ~15 minutes of your time.

## 6. BUILD CHALLENGE ⏱️ 15 min/day × 7

1. Run the **7-day operations loop** (standup every morning).
2. Log results daily; do the **end-of-week review** on day 7.
3. Mark **"AI Business System ✅"** in MY AI COMPANY.

## 7. RESULT

A **running AI business system**: it plans, produces, and sells daily — with you approving, not
grinding. You can feel it operating.
