# 🎮 LEVEL 3 — Let AI design a product

**🎯 CHALLENGE:** Can AI create something people actually want — in one hour?
**🏆 REWARD:** Your first digital product (Product Creator employee #3).

---

## 1. WHY THIS MATTERS

A business with no product is just an idea. Today you get a real, sellable thing. Digital products
are the cheat code for beginners: you make them once with AI, they cost €0 to copy, and the margin
is basically 100%. No inventory, no shipping. You'll have a finished product before bed.

## 2. WATCH IT IN ACTION

The Product Creator turns "aesthetic study planner" into a complete spec: the pages, the sections,
the exact text inside, plus a name and what makes it worth €9. You drop that into Canva or Notion,
spend 30 minutes making it pretty, and you've got a product. One hour, idea → asset.

## 3. COPY THIS PROMPT

```
You are my Product Creator — a senior digital-product designer on my team.
MISSION: turn an idea into a finished, sellable digital product I can make today with free 
tools (Canva, Notion, Google Docs) — no code.
PRODUCT IDEA: an aesthetic study & productivity planner for Gen Z students. (Replace with 
YOUR product idea.)

Deliver a complete build kit:
1. Product name (3 options) + a 1-line promise.
2. The full structure — every page/section, in order.
3. The actual text/content to put inside each section (write it for me, ready to paste).
4. The look: colors, font vibe, and layout notes so it looks premium.
5. Which free tool to build it in and exact steps.
6. A fair price for beginners, and a "premium version" idea I can upsell later.
End with a 6-step checklist to finish it in under an hour.
```

## 4. PASTE IT INTO CLOUD AI

1. Open your AI chat / Project. Paste the prompt.
2. Replace the **PRODUCT IDEA** line with your Level 2 quick win.
3. Send. The AI writes the *entire* product for you.

## 5. EXPECTED RESULT

A full product blueprint *with the content already written* — names, sections, the words inside,
the design vibe, the tool, and a finish-in-an-hour checklist. You're not creating from scratch;
you're assembling.

## 6. BUILD CHALLENGE ⏱️ 60 min

1. Open **Canva** (or **Notion**) and build the product using the AI's structure + text.
2. Export it (PDF, or a Notion share link / template).
3. Save the file in your MY AI COMPANY doc under **"Assets Built → Product #1 ✅"**.

## 7. RESULT

You own a **finished digital product**, ready to sell. (Yes — already.)
