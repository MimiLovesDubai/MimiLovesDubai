# 🧰 Kit #02 — AI TikTok Agency

**1. Business model:** Agency / monthly retainer (recurring income).
**2. Niche:** Faceless short-form content for small brands & local businesses that "don't have time."
**3. Target audience:** Café owners, coaches, small e-com brands who want TikTok/Reels but won't film.
**4. Offers:** Starter — "10 ready-to-post videos" (€99 one-off). Premium — "Done-for-you content,
20 videos/month" (€399/mo retainer).
**5. AI team:** Trend Hunter → Content Creator → TikTok Agent → Sales Agent → Customer Support.
**6. Prompts:**
- Content: *"Make 20 short-form video concepts (hook + script + caption + hashtags) for [client's niche]."*
- Sales: *"Write a cold DM + a 1-page offer to pitch a TikTok content retainer to a local [business]."*
- Ops: *"Turn these 20 concepts into a weekly posting calendar."*
**7. Workflows:** Claude (scripts) → CapCut/Canva (edit) → client's TikTok → Airtable (content calendar) → invoice via Gumroad/Stripe.
**8. First product:** A free "3-video sample pack" for one local business to win the deal.
**9. First customer strategy:** DM 10 local businesses with a free sample. First move: make a sample
for the one with the weakest current content and show the before/after.
**10. 30-day challenge:** W1 build your sample + offer · W2 pitch 20 businesses · W3 land 1 retainer
+ deliver · W4 systemize delivery, add client #2.
