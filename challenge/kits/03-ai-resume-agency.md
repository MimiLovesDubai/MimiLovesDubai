# 🧰 Kit #03 — AI Resume & LinkedIn Agency

**1. Business model:** Service / per-order (fast cash, repeatable).
**2. Niche:** Resume + LinkedIn glow-ups for students and early-career job seekers.
**3. Target audience:** Gen Z grads and career-switchers stressed about standing out.
**4. Offers:** Starter — "AI Resume Rewrite" (€19). Premium — "Resume + LinkedIn + cover letter
+ interview prep" (€59).
**5. AI team:** Product Creator (the service delivery) → Brand Director → Content Creator → Sales Agent.
**6. Prompts:**
- Delivery: *"Rewrite this resume for a [role], punchy and ATS-friendly; keep it truthful, only reword and restructure."*
- Content: *"10 TikTok hooks about resume mistakes Gen Z makes + 3 scripts."*
- Sales: *"Write a post + DM offering a €19 resume rewrite, with a free '3 fixes' teaser."*
**7. Workflows:** Client sends resume → Claude (rewrite) → Canva (template) → deliver PDF → Gumroad checkout.
**8. First product:** A free "Top 3 fixes on your resume" mini-audit to win the first client.
**9. First customer strategy:** Post resume-fix content; offer free mini-audits in grad groups and
LinkedIn comments. First move: audit 3 resumes free, ask for a testimonial, then charge.
**10. 30-day challenge:** W1 build templates + offer · W2 free audits → paid upgrades · W3 collect
testimonials + raise price · W4 add the €59 premium bundle.

> ⚖️ Keep it honest: rewrite and restructure real experience — never invent qualifications.
