# 🧰 AI BUSINESS KIT — Template

> Copy this template to spin up any of the 50 kits. Each kit is a "company in a box": point the
> Challenge's AI employees at it and run Levels 1–10. Fill every section.

**Kit #__ — [BUSINESS NAME]**

### 1. Business model
One line on how it makes money (e.g. digital products, agency/retainer, affiliate, ads).

### 2. Niche
The specific corner of the market (specific > broad).

### 3. Target audience
Who buys, and the exact pain/desire they have.

### 4. Offers
- **Starter offer** (€__): the easy first buy.
- **Premium offer** (€__): the upsell/bundle.

### 5. AI team (which employees to hire)
Which of the 16 AI employees this kit needs first (e.g. Trend Hunter → Product Creator → Content Creator → Sales Agent).

### 6. Prompts (the 3 that matter most)
- Product prompt: …
- Content prompt: …
- Sales prompt: …

### 7. Workflows (no-code)
The simple flow: tool → tool → tool (e.g. Claude → Canva → Gumroad → MailerLite).

### 8. First product
The exact thing to make in Level 3.

### 9. First customer strategy
Where they hang out + the first non-spammy move to get sale #1.

### 10. 30-day challenge
A simple weekly target: Week 1 build, Week 2 launch, Week 3 sell, Week 4 scale.
