# 🧰 Kit #01 — AI Study Notes & Aesthetic Templates

**1. Business model:** Digital products (sell once, copy free, ~100% margin).
**2. Niche:** Aesthetic study & productivity templates for students.
**3. Target audience:** Gen Z students who want to study prettier, focus better, get organized.
**4. Offers:** Starter — a single Notion/PDF planner (€9). Premium — "Ultimate Student Bundle"
(planner + revision templates + wallpapers, €29).
**5. AI team:** Trend Hunter → Product Creator → Brand Director → Content Creator → Sales Agent.
**6. Prompts:**
- Product: *"Create an aesthetic study planner for Gen Z students — full structure + the text inside each section, ready for Canva/Notion."*
- Content: *"10 TikTok hooks showing a study planner glow-up + 3 scripts."*
- Sales: *"Write a launch post + 3 DM scripts for my €9 student planner (Gumroad link)."*
**7. Workflows:** Claude → Canva/Notion (build) → Gumroad (sell) → MailerLite (email) → TikTok (traffic).
**8. First product:** One aesthetic weekly study planner (PDF or Notion template).
**9. First customer strategy:** Post a "study with me / planner reveal" video; drop the link in
study TikToks, r/GetStudying, and student Discords. First move: reply helpfully in 3 study threads
with a free mini-template.
**10. 30-day challenge:** W1 build planner + brand · W2 launch on Gumroad + post daily · W3 add the
bundle + email funnel · W4 scale content, aim for €1,000.
