Dear Gran Meliá Dubai Jumeirah team,

Congratulations on bringing the first Gran Meliá to Dubai.

I'm Mariska Beenen of Gallery Dutch Art, an Amsterdam studio creating bespoke art and collections for hospitality. Inspired by the hotel's seafront setting at Port de La Mer, we prepared a short concept, "Two Waters": a collection that connects the reflected light of Dutch canals with the light of the Gulf, with a signature pair at the arrival, a suite collection of originals and Living Paintings that can be programmed for events.

You can view the seven-page concept here:
https://3ae99428-3e18-4c94-b6c6-2d8add59bbf8.usrfiles.com/ugd/d8a765_ff3e5a23f27e43738766b3cc37f4b51c.pdf

It is unsolicited and only a starting point; for the opening season and beyond, we would adapt it entirely to your brief.

Would you be open to a twenty-minute conversation in the coming weeks?
