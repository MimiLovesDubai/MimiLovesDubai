The Gallery

One creative partner for the spaces that define a destination.

Gallery Dutch Art is an art gallery based in Amsterdam, working internationally. We create distinctive art collections, visual identities and immersive experiences for luxury hospitality and landmark developments.

From a single statement artwork to the complete visual identity of a resort, we work alongside owners, developers, architects and interior studios — from first concept to installation. Private collectors can acquire original works and limited digital editions.
