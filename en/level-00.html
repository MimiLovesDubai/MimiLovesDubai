LEVEL 0

What if AI was your first employee?

🎯 CHALLENGE: Can you start building a company with AI before most people even understand what's happening? 🏆 REWARD: Your company HQ + your first digital employee.

💡 1. WHY THIS MATTERS

For the first time in history, one person can run a whole company — because AI can be your *team*, not just a tool. A team you can summon in seconds, that never sleeps, never complains, and works for cents. The people who realize this *right now* get a head start the rest of the world hasn't caught up to yet. That's you. Tonight you stop being "someone learning AI" and become the founder.

You don't need money, code, or experience. You need a laptop, a free AI account, and the next 20 minutes.

👀 2. WATCH IT IN ACTION

A student opens Claude, types *"Act as my business strategist and find me a business I can start with AI this week."* Sixty seconds later they have 5 ideas, a recommended pick, and a first step. No meeting. No agency. No $5,000. That's one employee. You're about to hire your first.

📋 3. COPY THIS PROMPT

You are my AI Strategist — the first employee in my new one-person, AI-run company. 
You are sharp, honest, and allergic to fluff. You think like a founder who has launched 
many small online businesses.

Your job: help me build a real online business in 30 days using only AI and free no-code 
tools — with no coding and no big budget.

Here is what I want you to do right now:
1. In 3 sentences, explain what a "one-person AI company" actually is, like I'm 15.
2. Ask me 3 quick questions about me (my interests, time, and what I'd enjoy selling) so 
   you can tailor everything that comes next.
Keep it short, hype me up a little, and end by telling me we start the moment I answer.

🖱️ 4. PASTE IT INTO CLOUD AI

  1. Go to claude.ai (or chatgpt.com) and create a free account.
  2. Click New chat.
  3. Paste the prompt above and hit enter.
  4. Answer the 3 questions it asks you. (Be honest — this shapes your whole company.)

✨ 5. EXPECTED RESULT

The AI replies as your Strategist: a punchy explanation of your AI company, then 3 tailored questions. When you answer, it locks in *who you are* — the foundation for every level after this.

🛠️ 6. BUILD CHALLENGE ⏱️ 15 min

  1. Create a free page in Notion (or a Google Doc) and name it "MY AI COMPANY 🏢".
  2. Add three headings: Employees Hired, Assets Built, Money In.
  3. Under *Employees Hired*, paste the prompt above and write: #1 — AI Strategist ✅.
  4. Sign up free for Claude (claude.ai). This is your HQ.

📦 7. RESULT

You now have: a company HQ doc, a free AI workspace, and employee #1 (your AI Strategist) who knows who you are.
