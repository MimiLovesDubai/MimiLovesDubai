LEVEL 7

The First Sale Challenge

🎯 CHALLENGE: Get your first paying customer. Real money. Real proof. 🏆 REWARD: Proof the system works (Sales Agent, employee #11).

💡 1. WHY THIS MATTERS

This is the level that changes your life — not because of the money (it might be €9), but because of what it *proves*: strangers will pay you. Once that's real, everything else is just turning a dial up. Today we set up a way to get paid in 10 minutes (no code) and we go get sale #1.

👀 2. WATCH IT IN ACTION

You upload your product to Gumroad (free), set the price, and get a link. Your Sales Agent writes the post and the DM script that turns interest into a checkout. Someone clicks, pays, and your phone buzzes: *"You made a sale."* That sound is the whole game.

📋 3. COPY THIS PROMPT

You are my Sales Agent — a friendly, no-pressure closer on my team.
MISSION: turn attention into my first sale, ethically (no spam, no fake scarcity).
MY PRODUCT + PRICE + LINK: [paste product, price, and your Gumroad link]
MY AUDIENCE: Gen Z students. (Replace.)

Give me:
1. A "launch post" announcing the product (for TikTok caption + IG + a story) that makes 
   people want it without being cringe.
2. 3 DM scripts: (a) someone who liked my content, (b) a friend/peer, (c) a community 
   member who has the problem I solve. Warm, human, short.
3. A simple 1-line reply for the 3 most common objections ("too expensive", "does it 
   work?", "I'll buy later").
4. A 24-hour "first sale" action plan: exactly what to post/send and when.
End with the single first message I should send right now.

🖱️ 4. PASTE IT INTO CLOUD AI

  1. First, set up checkout: go to gumroad.com, create a free account, New product, upload

your Level 3 product, set a price, Publish, copy the link.

  1. Paste the prompt with your product + price + link. Send.
  2. Send the first message the Sales Agent gives you.

✨ 5. EXPECTED RESULT

A launch post, human DM scripts, objection one-liners, and a 24-hour plan — plus your live Gumroad checkout. Everything needed to get sale #1.

🛠️ 6. BUILD CHALLENGE ⏱️ 60 min

  1. Publish your product on Gumroad (live link).
  2. Post the launch post and send 5 DMs from the scripts.
  3. Log every reply in MY AI COMPANY under "Money In". (Tip: offer a small launch discount to

your first 5 — speed > price.)

📦 7. RESULT

A live store, an active launch, and your shots taken at sale #1. (Many get it within 48h.)
