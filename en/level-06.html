LEVEL 6

Build your AI management team

🎯 CHALLENGE: Can you stop doing everything yourself and let an AI team run the departments? 🏆 REWARD: AI Workforce unlocked (CEO + 3 Directors).

💡 1. WHY THIS MATTERS

Until now *you* ran every employee. That doesn't scale. Real companies have managers who think and delegate. You're about to promote your business into a real org: an AI CEO that sets direction and tells you what to do each day, plus a Marketing, Sales, and Product Director that run their departments. This is the moment your side project becomes a *company*.

👀 2. WATCH IT IN ACTION

You ask the AI CEO: *"What should the company do today?"* It checks your goal, looks at what's done, and replies: *"Marketing: post videos 2 & 3. Sales: DM 5 leads with this script. Product: prep the premium upsell."* You went from "what do I even do?" to a daily plan in 10 seconds.

📋 3. COPY THIS PROMPT

You are my AI CEO — the leader of my one-person, AI-run company. You manage a team of AI 
directors and keep us focused on making money, simply and ethically.

MY COMPANY: [paste your business + brand + offer]
MY GOAL: get my first paying customers in the next 7 days.
MY TEAM: Marketing Director, Sales Director, Product Director (you delegate to them).

Do this:
1. Set our ONE priority for the week in a single sentence.
2. Give me today's plan as 3 tasks max (one per director), each with the exact action.
3. For each director, write their job-description prompt (so I can "activate" them in a 
   chat when I need them) in this format:
   "You are my [Director]. Mission: ___. You always return: ___."
4. Tell me how to report back to you each day so you can give me the next plan.
Keep me focused. If I'm overcomplicating, call it out. End with today's first task.

🖱️ 4. PASTE IT INTO CLOUD AI

  1. Paste; fill in your company and goal lines. Send.
  2. Copy each Director job-description the CEO writes into your MY AI COMPANY doc.
  3. Best setup: make a Claude Project (or Custom GPT) called *"AI CEO"* with this prompt, so

your CEO remembers everything. Do daily check-ins there.

✨ 5. EXPECTED RESULT

A weekly priority, a 3-task daily plan, and ready-to-use prompts for your Marketing, Sales, and Product Directors. You now have a management team that tells *you* what to do.

🛠️ 6. BUILD CHALLENGE ⏱️ 30 min

  1. Set up your AI CEO (Project/GPT) and run your first daily check-in.
  2. Save all 4 managers (CEO + 3 Directors) under Employees Hired (#7–#10).
  3. Do today's first task the CEO gives you.

📦 7. RESULT

A working AI management team running Marketing, Sales, and Product — with you as founder.
