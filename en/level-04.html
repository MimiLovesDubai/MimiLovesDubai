LEVEL 4

Let AI build a brand

🎯 CHALLENGE: Can AI create a brand that looks like a real company, not a side project? 🏆 REWARD: Complete business identity (Brand Director, employee #4).

💡 1. WHY THIS MATTERS

People don't buy from "some random." They buy from brands they *feel* something about. A name, a vibe, a clear promise — that's what turns a PDF into a product worth paying for. The good news: AI is genuinely great at this, and you'll have a full identity in 30 minutes.

👀 2. WATCH IT IN ACTION

You feed AI your product and audience. It returns: brand name *"deskglow"*, the tagline *"study pretty, focus harder"*, a personality (calm, aesthetic, a little hype), colors, a logo concept, and a one-line offer. Drop the logo concept into Canva's free logo maker and you look legit instantly.

📋 3. COPY THIS PROMPT

You are my Brand Director — a senior brand strategist on my team.
MISSION: give my business a complete identity that looks like a real, trusted company.
MY BUSINESS: I sell aesthetic study & productivity digital products to Gen Z students. 
(Replace with YOUR business.)

Deliver:
1. 5 brand name options (short, memorable, available-sounding) + my favorite and why.
2. A tagline (3 options).
3. Brand personality in 3 words + the tone of voice.
4. Color palette (give hex codes) + font vibe.
5. A logo concept I can make in Canva (describe it simply).
6. My core OFFER in one irresistible sentence (what they get + the result + the price).
7. A 2-line "about" I can paste on my shop and socials.
Keep it cohesive, modern, and Gen Z. End by asking which name I'm choosing.

🖱️ 4. PASTE IT INTO CLOUD AI

  1. Paste into your AI chat / Project; swap the MY BUSINESS line.
  2. Send. Pick your name and tagline.
  3. Open Canva → Logo and recreate the logo concept (free templates + the colors/fonts given).

✨ 5. EXPECTED RESULT

A full brand kit: name, tagline, personality, colors (with hex codes), a logo concept, your offer, and an "about." Your side project now looks like a company.

🛠️ 6. BUILD CHALLENGE ⏱️ 30 min

  1. Lock your name + tagline + offer.
  2. Make a simple logo in Canva using the concept and colors.
  3. Save it all in MY AI COMPANY under "BRAND".

📦 7. RESULT

You have a complete brand identity — name, look, voice, and offer — ready to sell behind.
