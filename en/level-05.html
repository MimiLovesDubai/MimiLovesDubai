LEVEL 5

Can AI find customers?

🎯 CHALLENGE: Can AI build you a customer-acquisition machine? 🏆 REWARD: Lead Hunter + Content Creator (employees #5 & #6).

💡 1. WHY THIS MATTERS

A product nobody sees makes €0. This is where most people quit — they don't know how to get attention. You're going to hire two employees that solve it: a Content Creator that makes attention (posts that pull people in) and a Lead Hunter that finds where your customers already hang out. Distribution is the real game. Now you have a team for it.

👀 2. WATCH IT IN ACTION

The Content Creator turns your brand into 30 TikTok/Reel ideas — hooks, scripts, captions, hashtags — in one pass. The Lead Hunter lists the exact subreddits, TikTok hashtags, Discords, and student groups where your buyers scroll. You stop shouting into the void and start showing up where they are.

📋 3. COPY THIS PROMPT

You are TWO employees on my team working together. 

EMPLOYEE A — Content Creator: make attention.
EMPLOYEE B — Lead Hunter: find where my customers already are.

MY BRAND/OFFER: [paste your brand + one-line offer from Level 4]
MY CUSTOMER: Gen Z students who want to study prettier and focus better. (Replace.)

Content Creator, give me:
- 10 short-form video hooks (the first line that stops the scroll)
- 3 full video scripts (hook + 20–30s + caption + 6 hashtags)
- 5 "post this to sell without being cringe" caption ideas

Lead Hunter, give me:
- 8 exact places online where my customers hang out (subreddits, TikTok hashtags, 
  Discords, student communities) 
- 1 non-spammy way to show up in each
- 1 free "lead magnet" I could give away to collect emails

End by telling me the ONE post to make first.

🖱️ 4. PASTE IT INTO CLOUD AI

  1. Paste; fill in your brand/offer and customer lines.
  2. Send. You'll get content + a map of where to post it.
  3. Save both as Employees #5 (Lead Hunter) and #6 (Content Creator).

✨ 5. EXPECTED RESULT

A pile of ready-to-post content *and* a list of exact places to post it — plus the single first post to make. That's a customer-acquisition machine, on day one.

🛠️ 6. BUILD CHALLENGE ⏱️ 45 min

  1. Make the first post the AI told you to (film it simply, or use a Canva/text video).
  2. Post it in one of the Lead Hunter's locations.
  3. Log it in MY AI COMPANY under "Money In → Funnel started".

📦 7. RESULT

You have content, a place to put it, and your first post live — real attention incoming.
