LEVEL 1

Give AI its first mission

🎯 CHALLENGE: Can AI generate a real business idea — one *you'd* actually run? 🏆 REWARD: Your AI Opportunity Finder + your chosen business.

💡 1. WHY THIS MATTERS

Most people never start because they're stuck on "what business?" You're going to skip that pain entirely: your AI Strategist will generate ideas *matched to you*, score them, and pick a winner — in minutes. The goal isn't a perfect idea. It's a good-enough idea you can start tonight and improve as you go. Done beats perfect, always.

👀 2. WATCH IT IN ACTION

Ask AI for "5 AI-run businesses I can start with no code," and it returns options like: an aesthetic digital-product store for students, a faceless TikTok channel, a resume agency. It scores each on speed-to-money, fun, and how fast you can launch — then recommends one. Our running demo will use "aesthetic study & productivity digital products for students" (Notion templates + wallpapers, sold on Gumroad). Pick yours, or steal the demo.

📋 3. COPY THIS PROMPT

You are my AI Opportunity Finder. Based on everything you know about me from our chat, 
generate 5 online businesses I can start THIS WEEK using only AI and free no-code tools 
(no coding, small budget).

For each idea give me:
- Name of the business (1 line)
- What it sells and to whom
- Why it fits ME specifically
- How fast it could make its first €1 (1 = slow, 5 = fast)
- How fun it is for me (1–5)
- First product I could sell

Then: score each idea, pick the single BEST one for a beginner who wants money fast, and 
explain in 3 sentences why. End by asking if I want to lock it in or reroll.

🖱️ 4. PASTE IT INTO CLOUD AI

  1. Open the same chat with your AI Strategist (so it remembers you).
  2. Paste the prompt and send.
  3. Read the 5 ideas. Reply "lock it in" for the pick, or "reroll" for 5 fresh ones.

✨ 5. EXPECTED RESULT

A scored list of 5 tailored businesses and one clear recommendation with a first product. You'll feel the "oh, I could actually do this one" click. That's the signal — lock it in.

🛠️ 6. BUILD CHALLENGE ⏱️ 20 min

  1. Run the prompt and choose your business (reroll until one excites you).
  2. In your MY AI COMPANY doc, add a section "THE BUSINESS" and paste the winner.
  3. Write your business in one line: *"I sell ___ to ___ using AI."*

📦 7. RESULT

You now have a chosen business and a one-line pitch — the target everything else aims at.
