# Bonus — Creative & viral agent businesses (Gen Z edition)

> Goal: fun, viral, creative agent businesses that actually make money. For each idea you'll see
> concretely **how it earns** — because going viral is fun, but the goal is revenue.

Companion code: [`code/viral_content_agent.py`](../code/viral_content_agent.py).

---

## Honest first: views ≠ money (but they lead to it)

Going viral isn't a business model by itself. Money comes from what you put behind the views: a
product, a service, affiliate, or brands paying for your reach. The agents below make the creative
work scalable; you pick the revenue model and keep posting consistently. Always respect platform
rules — no fake engagement, no deception. Real value wins.

> 💡 **Fastest to real money:** digital products and print-on-demand (you sell something right
> away), then affiliate/UGC. Pure ad revenue from views takes the longest.

---

## The content studio (the code)

`viral_content_agent.py` is a small team of agents that makes viral short-form content. Give it a
niche and you get ready-to-use concepts: an **ideas agent** invents scroll-stopping videos, and a
**script agent** delivers, per idea, the hook, the script, the on-screen text, the caption and the
hashtags. Run it:

```bash
python code/viral_content_agent.py "weird history facts" 5
```

All that's left is to film/edit (or plug in an AI image or video generator) and post daily. Below
are six ways to make money with it.

## 6 creative agent businesses that go viral

### 1. Faceless short-form channel 🎬
A channel without your face (weird facts, study aesthetics, "AI explains X", scary stories). The
agent delivers daily concepts; you edit and post.
- **How it earns:** affiliate links in your bio, UGC deals (brands pay you to make content),
  sponsored posts, and later creator funds/ads. You can also sell content packs to other creators.
- **Starter prompt:** *"Come up with 7 faceless short-form video ideas about [niche] with a strong
  hook and a clear affiliate angle to [product]."*

### 2. Aesthetic digital products 🎨
Notion templates, phone wallpapers, Lightroom presets, study planners, CapCut templates. The agent
designs concepts + sales copy + SEO for your shop.
- **How it earns:** direct sales on **Gumroad or Etsy**, €5–€29 each, near-100% margin. Your viral
  posts drive people to your shop. **Fastest real revenue.**
- **Starter prompt:** *"Come up with 5 sellable Notion templates for [audience], with a title, sales
  copy and 5 SEO keywords each."*

### 3. AI persona / virtual creator 👾
A recurring AI character with a recognizable vibe that posts daily (quotes, mini-stories, art). You
make the consistent look with an image generator; the agent writes the content.
- **How it earns:** brand collaborations, a Patreon/Ko-fi with extras, and your own merch.
- **Starter prompt:** *"Create an AI persona for [niche]: name, vibe, tone, and 7 post ideas that
  fit the character."*

### 4. Meme / relatable page as a service 😂
Run a meme page, or make memes for brands that want to reach a young audience.
- **How it earns:** sponsorship of your page, or a **monthly retainer** to make memes for a brand
  (recurring income).
- **Starter prompt:** *"Come up with 10 relatable meme concepts for [audience] about [theme], with a
  caption idea — not cringe, very shareable."*

### 5. Print-on-demand designs 👕
The agent invents sticker, T-shirt and poster designs + listings; you connect a POD service
(Printful, Redbubble) that prints and ships.
- **How it earns:** margin per item sold, with no inventory. Your viral posts are free advertising
  for your shop.
- **Starter prompt:** *"Come up with 8 sticker designs for [niche] with a short slogan and a product
  listing (title + tags)."*

### 6. Micro-tool / "glow-up" generator ✨
A small paid tool: a caption generator, bio/rizz helper, "your aura" description, playlist names,
or aesthetic quote maker. Build it with the Claude API (module 04).
- **How it earns:** a small amount per use or a one-time purchase on Gumroad, or free as a
  lead-magnet to a bigger service.
- **Starter prompt:** *"Write the system prompt for a tool that turns [input] into [fun output] for
  [audience], with a consistent, funny tone."*

## Plug in images and video (optional but powerful)

The agents deliver the text and the plan. For the visuals you can connect an AI image or video
generator, so your whole pipeline — idea, script, visual — is automated. Combine that with the
blueprint from the capstone module and you have a content business that largely runs itself.

## The honest rules

- **Consistency wins.** One viral video is luck; posting every day is a strategy.
- **Distribution is the work.** Making content has become easy; standing out is the challenge.
- **Play fair.** No fake views, no stolen content, no deception — it backfires (and breaks the law,
  module 11).
- **Pick one revenue model** and build toward it; don't mix everything at once.
